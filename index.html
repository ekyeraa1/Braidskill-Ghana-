Why BraidSkills?

Training should feel within reach.

Some young people want to learn a skill but find the cost of training and materials difficult to manage. BraidSkills Ghana aims to make hair-braiding training more affordable so more young people can explore a path towards earning an income.

₵

The proposed offer

One practical skill can open a door.

The programme is being designed around practical hair-braiding skills and the confidence to use them. Final training length, materials, and trainer arrangements will be confirmed after feedback and planning.

  • Guided hair-braiding practice
  • Introduction to customer care and pricing
  • Basic ideas for finding customers
  • Encouragement to explore self-employment

The exact techniques and materials included will be decided during programme planning.

Proposed training fee
GH₵500

A target fee intended to be more affordable for young people in Berekum.

  • Training fee details to be confirmed
  • Tools and materials included only if the final budget allows
  • Registration of interest does not guarantee a place

FAQs

Questions you may have.

Is the programme running now?

No. It is a proposed programme that is still being developed.

Is the fee definitely GH₵500?

GH₵500 is the proposed target fee. It must be reviewed against trainer, tool, and material costs.

Will tools and materials be provided?

This has not yet been confirmed. The final plan will explain what is included in the fee.

Who is the programme for?

It is intended for young people in Berekum who want to learn practical hair-braiding skills.
